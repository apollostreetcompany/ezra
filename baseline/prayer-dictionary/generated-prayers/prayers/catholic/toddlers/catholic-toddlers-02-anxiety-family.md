# For a Toddler Who Feels Afraid

Lord Jesus, this little one is afraid, and fear feels very big in a small body. Help us not to scold fear, but to bring it to You. Thank You for family arms, familiar voices, and the grace of being loved before we can explain ourselves. Calm this child’s heart. Teach us to give steady comfort, not rushed impatience. May our home feel like a small shelter of Your Church, where mercy is practiced in ordinary rooms. Stay with us now, one breath and one moment at a time. Amen.
