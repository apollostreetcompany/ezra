# For a Toddler Learning Thanks

Good Father, this morning is full of gifts this child did not earn: light at the window, breakfast, clean clothes, toys on the floor, and arms ready to hold. Thank You for grace before we know how to ask. Lord Jesus, teach this little one to receive love and then give a little love back. When grabbing, whining, or forgetting thanks comes first, meet us with mercy and help us begin again. Holy Spirit, make praise small and real today: kind hands, quick sorry, shared toys, and a happy amen with the whole Church. Let our home answer Your goodness with obedient love. Amen.
