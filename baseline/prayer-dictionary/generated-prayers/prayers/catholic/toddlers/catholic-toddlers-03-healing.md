# For a Sick Toddler

Father, this little body is hurting, and the house feels smaller around the fever, cough, or tears. Thank You for breath, blankets, medicine, and the people who know how to help. Lord Jesus, You welcomed children when others wanted them sent away; welcome this child now with healing mercy. If we need a doctor, guide that care; if we need patience, give it before we run out. Holy Spirit, make this room calm and keep fear from ruling us. Let our Church's prayers surround this child. Teach us gratitude for every small comfort and gentle obedience until strength returns. Amen.
