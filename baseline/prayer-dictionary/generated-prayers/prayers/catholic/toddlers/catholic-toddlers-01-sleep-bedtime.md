# For a Toddler at Bedtime

Father, bedtime has come, and this little child is tired but not yet ready to let go. Thank You for the food, play, stories, and hands that carried us through the day. Where we were fussy, impatient, or afraid, give us mercy and teach us to say sorry and forgive. Lord Jesus, keep watch beside this bed and gather this baptized child close to Your Church. Holy Spirit, quiet the room, soften the heart, and help us rest in the Father's care. May tomorrow find us grateful, obedient in small things, and ready to love. Amen.
