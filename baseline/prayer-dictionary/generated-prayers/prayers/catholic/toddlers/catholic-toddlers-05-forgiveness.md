# For a Toddler Learning Sorry

Lord Jesus, little hearts are learning love. Sometimes hands grab, feet stomp, and words come out loud. Help us teach sorry without shame and forgiveness without pretending nothing happened. Give this child grace to try again: to return the toy, give a hug if welcome, speak gently, or rest when tired. Forgive us grownups when our patience is thin. Holy Spirit, make our home a place where mercy is practiced early and often, because You first show mercy to us. Amen.
