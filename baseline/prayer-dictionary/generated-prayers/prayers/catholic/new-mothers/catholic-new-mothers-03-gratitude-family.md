# For a New Mother Giving Thanks for Family

Father, thank You for this family, not as an idea but as these actual people with needs, noises, limits, and gifts. Thank You for the child in my care, for the hands that help, for the meals and messages, and for the hidden graces I overlook when I am tired. Keep gratitude from becoming pressure to feel happy every minute. True thanks can include tears. Lord Jesus, bless this household and teach us to belong to one another without possessing one another. Let my small acts today become participation in Your patient love: feeding, washing, listening, forgiving, and beginning again. Holy Spirit, guard our home from resentment and comparison. Give us the virtues that make family life holy: patience, courage, tenderness, truth, and humor. I offer this day back to You, grateful not because it is easy, but because You are present in it. Amen.
