# For a New Mother Offering Praise

Father, my praise today may not sound impressive. It may be whispered over laundry, breathed during a feeding, or offered while I am too tired to finish a thought. Receive it anyway. Praise is telling the truth about You, not proving that I am serene. You are Creator, Redeemer, and Giver of life. Lord Jesus, unite these hidden hours to Your own self-giving love. Let me surrender the wish to be admired and receive the quieter vocation of being faithful. Holy Spirit, make my ordinary body and ordinary work a living offering. Keep me from despising smallness. The Church has always been carried by hidden prayers, unseen sacrifices, and grace moving beneath common tasks. Add my little yes to that great communion. I praise You by trusting You with this child, this day, and the mother You are still forming in me. Amen.
