# For a New Mother Asking Forgiveness

Lord, I have been sharper than I wanted to be. Tiredness explains some things, but it does not make lovelessness holy. Give me honest contrition without the useless drama of self-hatred. If I have spoken harshly to my child, partner, family, friend, or myself, forgive me and teach me repair. Let me not hide behind the sentence that this season is hard, even though it is. Let hardship become a place where grace works, not an excuse to stop choosing charity. Lord Jesus, meet me in confession, in prayer, and in the ordinary apology I may need to make today. Holy Spirit, strengthen my will before my feelings catch up. Help me turn: to lower my voice, ask for help sooner, rest when possible, and receive mercy as a daughter, not earn it as a performer. Amen.
