# For a New Mother Surrendering Anxiety

Father, I am holding a child and also holding a thousand fears. Some are reasonable duties; some are my attempt to become providence itself. Teach me the difference. I can feed, change, call the doctor, ask for help, and rest when rest is given. I cannot guarantee every future pain away from this child. Lord Jesus, You entered the world helpless and entrusted Yourself to human arms. Make my arms faithful without making my heart frantic. Holy Spirit, give me the grace of the next right thing, not the illusion of total control. Let the sacraments and prayers of the Church steady me when my thoughts scatter. Blessed Mother, who pondered joy and sorrow, pray that I may love without clutching. I surrender this child to the Father who loved before I did. Give me courage for this hour and peace for what remains hidden. Amen.
