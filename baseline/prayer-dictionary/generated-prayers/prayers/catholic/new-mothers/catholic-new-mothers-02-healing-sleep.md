# For a New Mother Needing Healing and Sleep

Lord Jesus, my body is tired in ways I did not know a body could be tired. I bring You the soreness, the interrupted sleep, the strange emotions, and the fear that needing help means failing. It does not. I am a creature, not an endless supply. Heal what needs healing in my body and mind. Give wisdom to those who care for me, and humility to me when I must receive care. Thank You for this child, and also for every cup of water, folded blanket, kind message, and hour of sleep You provide. Holy Spirit, quiet the accusations that grow louder at night. If the baby wakes, meet me there. If sleep comes, teach me to receive it without guilt. May this room be held inside Your mercy, and may tomorrow find me restored enough for the next faithful act of love. Amen.
