# For a Grandmother Giving Thanks

Father, I come with years behind me, and I know they are not only my achievement. They are mercy. Thank You for the children and grandchildren I have held in my arms and in my prayers, for meals cooked, stories repeated, work endured, sins forgiven, and joys I did not know enough to expect. Keep gratitude from becoming nostalgia that refuses the present. You are good now, not only then. Lord Jesus, receive my praise in the form it can take today: a blessing spoken, a candle lit, a call made, a burden quietly offered. Holy Spirit, make my memory honest and my hope young. Let the communion of saints remind me that no faithful love is wasted. I praise You for the road already traveled and offer You the road still before me, however long or short. Amen.
