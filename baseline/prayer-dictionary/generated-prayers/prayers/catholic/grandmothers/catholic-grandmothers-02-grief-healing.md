# For a Grandmother in Grief

Lord Jesus, grief at this age can feel crowded. One loss awakens others, and a quiet room can hold many names. Do not let me mistake this ache for abandonment. You have conquered death, yet You do not ask me to call death small. Teach me to grieve with Christian hope: honest about the wound, stubborn about the resurrection. Heal the places in me that have grown tired of losing people. Give me grace to speak love while I can, forgive what still binds me, and receive comfort without embarrassment. Father, gather the departed into Your mercy according to Your will, and keep me faithful among the living. Holy Spirit, turn memory into prayer, not torment. With the saints, let me wait for the day when Christ makes all things new. Until then, help me love the people still before me. Amen.
