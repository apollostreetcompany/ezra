# For a Grandmother at Night

Father, night asks me to let go. I can review the day, but I cannot reopen it. I can pray for my family, but I cannot keep watch over every house and heart. Teach me the peace of submission. Receive what I did faithfully, forgive what I did poorly, and bless what remains unfinished. Lord Jesus, into Your hands I place my body, my memories, my worries, and those I love. Let sleep be an act of trust, not a defeat. Holy Spirit, quiet the old fears that visit when the room grows still. Surround me with the prayers of the Church and the mercy of Christ. Whether tomorrow brings strength or weakness, tasks or waiting, let me wake ready to belong to You. For tonight, I rest as Your child. Amen.
