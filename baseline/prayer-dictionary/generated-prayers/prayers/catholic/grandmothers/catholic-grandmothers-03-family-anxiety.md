# For a Grandmother Worried About Her Family

Father, I worry about my family because I love them, but love and fear have become tangled in me. Show me the difference between prayer and control. I can listen, advise when asked, help where I am welcome, and keep loving. I cannot live another person’s life for them, and I cannot force grace into a heart by anxiety. Lord Jesus, You know each child and grandchild more truly than I do. Seek the wandering, strengthen the weary, correct the proud, and comfort the wounded. Holy Spirit, make my concern fruitful instead of restless. Let my words become fewer and kinder, my prayers more faithful, and my trust more real. If there is a next faithful thing for me, show it plainly. If there is only waiting, teach me to wait with the Church, with hope, and without despair. Amen.
