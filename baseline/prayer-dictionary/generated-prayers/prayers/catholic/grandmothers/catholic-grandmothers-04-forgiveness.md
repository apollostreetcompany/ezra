# For a Grandmother Seeking Forgiveness

Lord, age has not removed my need for mercy. I can still defend myself, rehearse old injuries, excuse sharp words, and call pride by gentler names. Search me without crushing me. Let me see the truth under Your mercy, not under the accusations of despair. If I have wounded my children, grandchildren, spouse, friend, or neighbor, give me courage to confess what is mine. If some repairs cannot be made as I wish, teach me to entrust even that sorrow to You. Lord Jesus, meet me with forgiveness that changes me, not merely comforts me. Holy Spirit, soften what has become rigid. Give me the humility to ask pardon and the charity to grant it. Let my later years become clearer, kinder, and more available to grace than my former years. Amen.
