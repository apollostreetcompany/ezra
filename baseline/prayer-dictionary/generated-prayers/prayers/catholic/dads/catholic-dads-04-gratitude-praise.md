# For a Dad Giving Thanks

God my Father, gratitude tells the truth about my life. I did not create my own breath, my abilities, my children, my chances, or the mercy that has kept me from worse roads. Keep me from the pride that receives gifts and calls them achievements. I praise You for work to do, people to love, strength enough for today, and forgiveness when I have spent those gifts badly. Lord Jesus, teach me to stand before You as a creature, not a competitor. Let thanksgiving make me generous instead of self-satisfied. Holy Spirit, open my eyes to the quiet mercies in this house: shoes by the door, voices in another room, food on the table, another opportunity to bless rather than complain. May my praise become visible as service. I receive these gifts from You and offer them back through faithful love. Amen.
