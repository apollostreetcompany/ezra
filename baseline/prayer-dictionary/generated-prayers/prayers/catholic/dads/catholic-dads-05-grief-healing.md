# For a Dad Carrying Grief

Lord Jesus, grief has made me quieter than people know. I am tempted to think strength means keeping it sealed, but hidden sorrow can still shape the whole house. Teach me the difference between steadiness and refusal to grieve. You wept without ceasing to be Lord. Give me courage to mourn truthfully and to let those who love me see enough to love me back. Heal what grief has tightened in my body, my speech, and my prayers. Father, hold the one I miss in Your mercy, and hold me while I keep walking. Holy Spirit, keep sorrow from turning into anger or distance. Let the communion of saints enlarge my hope when death feels final. I do not ask to understand everything tonight. Give me enough hope in the resurrection to do tomorrow’s duties with tenderness. Amen.
