# For a Dad Repairing What He Has Hurt

Father, I have not always loved my family with the patience and courage they needed. Sometimes I have been absent while present, quick to correct and slow to listen, more concerned with being respected than being holy. Do not let me dress this up as stress or personality. Give me contrition that turns around. Lord Jesus, forgive me through Your mercy and teach me to repair without demanding that others heal on my schedule. If an apology is needed, make it plain, humble, and specific. If trust must be rebuilt, give me perseverance in small truthful acts. Holy Spirit, expose the pride that wants credit for admitting wrong. Let the grace of reconciliation reach the dinner table, the car ride, the bedtime conversation, and the quiet places where old patterns usually return. Make me a father who shows mercy because he has received it. Amen.
