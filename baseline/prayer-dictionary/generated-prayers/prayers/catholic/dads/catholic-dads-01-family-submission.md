# For a Dad Offering His Family to God

Father, I often say my family matters most, but I still try to run the day as if everything depends on my control. Teach me the difference between leadership and possession. My wife, my children, and my work are not trophies or burdens; they are persons and duties entrusted to me. Lord Jesus, make me strong in the way You are strong: truthful, patient, sacrificial, and not afraid to serve. Give me the humility to apologize quickly and the courage to set a holy direction without harshness. Let the grace I receive from You become protection for this home, not pressure on it. Holy Spirit, order my speech, my habits, my money, my attention, and my rest. Help me submit first to God, so that I do not demand from my family what only God can give. I offer this household to You again today. Amen.
