# For a Dad Under Pressure

Lord Jesus, I am anxious, and I often hide it under busyness, irritation, or jokes that keep people from asking too much. You see beneath that. Show me the difference between carrying my responsibilities and pretending to be the savior of my family. I can work honestly, plan wisely, protect where I am able, and ask for counsel. I cannot hold the future in my hands. Father, give daily bread, not because I have earned control, but because You are good. Holy Spirit, keep fear from making me hard. Let prayer interrupt the spiral before my anxiety leaks out as anger. Teach me to receive grace through the Church, the sacraments, and the ordinary help of people who love me. Give me enough light for the next faithful decision, and enough trust to leave hidden outcomes with You. Amen.
