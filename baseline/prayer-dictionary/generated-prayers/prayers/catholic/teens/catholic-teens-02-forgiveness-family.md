# For a Teenager Forgiving at Home

Lord Jesus, home is where I am loved, and sometimes where I am most easily wounded. I do not want to fake sweetness while keeping a private courtroom in my head. Show me the difference between excusing wrong and refusing revenge. If I need to speak honestly, give me courage without cruelty. If I need to be quiet, let it be charity, not sulking. You forgave from the Cross without calling sin harmless, and You invite me to share Your life, not merely admire it. Give me contrition for my own sharp words, and patience with the weakness of the people nearest me. Let the grace I receive in the sacraments become visible in the hallway, at the table, and behind a closed bedroom door. Holy Spirit, make one small act of peace possible today. I offer You my hurt before it becomes hatred. Amen.
