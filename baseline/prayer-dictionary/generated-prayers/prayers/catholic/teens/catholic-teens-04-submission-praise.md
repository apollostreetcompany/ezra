# For a Teenager Learning Surrender

Lord, I want You to bless my plans, but I do not always want You to lead them. That is the honest beginning. Praise is not only a song I like; it is admitting that You are God and I am not. Teach me the freedom of that truth. I offer You my classes, friends, body, online life, private thoughts, and the choices no one else sees. Keep me from treating obedience as a loss when it is really participation in the life of Christ. If Your will interrupts my mood, help me answer with faith before I feel ready. If praise feels dry, let me still tell the truth about Your goodness. Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, receive this day. Make me brave enough to be holy in ordinary places, cheerful enough to serve without being noticed, and humble enough to begin again. Amen.
