# For a Teenager Who Is Grieving

Lord Jesus, grief has made the world feel strange. Things keep moving, but something in me has stopped. Do not let me think faith means acting untouched. You wept, and so You are not ashamed of tears. Show me the difference between hope and pretending. Hope does not erase the empty place; it keeps the empty place from becoming the whole truth. Bring healing to my mind, my body, my memories, and my prayers. When people do not know what to say, help me receive imperfect kindness. When I am angry or numb, keep me near the Cross, where love suffered without ceasing to be love. I entrust the one I miss, and myself, to Your mercy. Let the communion of saints remind me that death is not stronger than You. Give me enough strength for today, and enough hope to believe that resurrection is real. Amen.
