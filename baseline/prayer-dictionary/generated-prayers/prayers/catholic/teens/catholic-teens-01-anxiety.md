# For a Teenager Carrying Anxiety

Father, I am anxious, and pretending I am not does not make me brave. My mind runs ahead of me, trying to solve tomorrow before I have obeyed You today. Teach me the difference between responsibility and control. I can study, speak truth, apologize, ask for help, and take the next step; I cannot make every person approve of me or every outcome bend my way. Lord Jesus, You know what fear feels like in a human body. Stay near me with the grace You give through Your Church, Your word, and the quiet help of the Holy Spirit. Let prayer become more real to me than panic. With Mary and all the saints praying before Your throne, help me do the next faithful thing without demanding the whole future first. Give me peace that is not denial, courage that is not showing off, and trust that can breathe. Amen.
