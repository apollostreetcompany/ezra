# For a Teenager at Bedtime

God my Father, the day is ending, and I am tempted either to replay every mistake or to distract myself until I feel nothing. Teach me a truer way to rest. Let me look at this day under Your mercy. Thank You for the good I noticed and the good I missed: a laugh, a meal, a finished task, a person who did not give up on me. Forgive what was selfish, lazy, dishonest, or proud. If I hurt someone, help me repair it when morning comes. If I was hurt, keep bitterness from hardening while I sleep. Lord Jesus, place this unfinished day inside Your finished love. Holy Spirit, quiet my body and order my thoughts. Let my room become a small school of trust, not a place where worry rehearses itself. I sleep as a baptized child held by grace, not as a soul left alone. Amen.
