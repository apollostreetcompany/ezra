# For Anxiety Before the Next Faithful Thing

Lord Jesus Christ, I am anxious about school, friendships, my body, my future, and the version of myself other people seem to be judging. Some of these fears are sensible warnings; some are only noise dressed up as wisdom. Teach me the difference. Fear is real, but it is not my lord. You passed through death and rose, so even the things I cannot control are not outside Your mercy. Father, hold what I keep trying to carry. Holy Spirit, give me watchfulness without panic, honesty without self-hatred, and courage for the next faithful thing. When my thoughts race, let the Jesus Prayer become a small doorway back to You. Help me answer one message truthfully, study one page, apologize once, breathe once, and not demand the whole future before I obey. Keep me in the life of the Church, where I am more than my mood. Have mercy on me and steady me today.
