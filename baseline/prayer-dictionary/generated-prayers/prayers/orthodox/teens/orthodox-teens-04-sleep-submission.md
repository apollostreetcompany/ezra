# For Bedtime Surrender After a Full Day

Lord Jesus Christ, the day is over, but my mind has not agreed to stop. I replay what I said, what I should have said, what I looked like, what tomorrow may require. Bring me into truth, not performance. If I sinned today, let me confess it without making excuses or turning shame into another mirror. If I did something good, let me receive it as grace, not as proof that I am better than someone else. Father, I give You my phone, my unfinished homework, my friendships, my worries, and my body. They are not safer in my grip than in Your hands. Holy Spirit, quiet the passions that wake up at night: envy, fear, lust, anger, and pride. Let my room become a small place of repentance and rest. Under the mercy of the risen Christ, teach me to sleep as one who is loved, forgiven, and not in charge of the universe.
