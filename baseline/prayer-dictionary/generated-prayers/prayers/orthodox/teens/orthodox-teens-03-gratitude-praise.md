# For Gratitude Without Pretending Life Is Easy

Holy Trinity, I want to be grateful, but I often treat gratitude like a mood that must arrive before I can pray. Teach me better. Gratitude is not pretending every class, friendship, family stress, or private insecurity is easy. It is seeing that my life is received, not self-invented. Father, thank You for breath, food, sleep, laughter, music, teachers, correction, and the people who keep loving me when I am hard to love. Lord Jesus, thank You for Your Cross and Resurrection, which make even small mercies part of a larger hope. Holy Spirit, open my eyes without making me fake. Let praise become attention: to an icon, to Scripture, to the Liturgy, to the person beside me, to the work in front of me. Guard me from comparison that steals joy. Today, let me say thank you quickly, complain less dramatically, and use my gifts as something offered back to You.
