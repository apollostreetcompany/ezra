# For Forgiving Someone at Home

Father, I do not feel forgiving toward the person in my family who has hurt, annoyed, or misunderstood me. I would rather rehearse the scene until I win it in my head. But winning an argument inside myself is not the same as being healed. Lord Jesus, You forgave from the Cross and rose without becoming bitter. Teach me to will this person's good even if warmth comes later. Do not let me call revenge honesty, or call silence peace when it is only stored anger. Holy Spirit, expose the passion in me that wants to be superior, and give me the humility to repent where I have also wounded others. If I need a boundary, make it clean and truthful, not cruel. If I need to speak, make my words few and brave. Keep our home under Your mercy. By the prayers of the saints who learned patience in ordinary rooms, help me choose one act of charity today.
