# For Grief and Healing That Takes Time

Lord Jesus, something hurts, and I cannot fix it by understanding it. I may be grieving a death, a divorce, a diagnosis, a lost friendship, or a version of life I thought I would have. Do not let me pretend I am fine just to make others comfortable. But also do not let pain become my whole name. You entered the grave and broke it from the inside. Because You are risen, grief is not denied, but it is not final. Father, heal what can be healed now, and give endurance where healing is slow. Holy Spirit, keep my heart from hardening into sarcasm or despair. Place me near people in the Church who can carry silence without trying to explain everything. Teach me to pray when I have few words. Let tears, therapy, medicine, confession, and friendship all become places where Your mercy can meet me. Today, help me do one small good thing while I wait for resurrection hope to grow.
