# For Grief, Age, and Healing

Holy Trinity, I bring You the griefs that gather with age: people I miss, strength I no longer have, homes that changed, names no longer called across the table, and pains that visit without asking. I do not want to become only a keeper of losses. Yet I also do not want to rush past love by pretending loss is small. Lord Jesus Christ, You are risen, and therefore the grave is not the final interpreter of my life. Father, heal what can still be healed in body, memory, and relationship. Holy Spirit, keep tenderness alive where sorrow could become bitterness. Let the communion of saints feel less like an idea and more like a household I am walking toward. Bless doctors, caregivers, medicine, and rest. Teach me to receive help without shame. Until You wipe away every tear, make me patient, grateful for today's mercies, and ready to love the person in front of me.
