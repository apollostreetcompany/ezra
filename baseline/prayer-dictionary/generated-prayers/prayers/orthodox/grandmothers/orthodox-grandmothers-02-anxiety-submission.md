# For Surrendering Family Worry

Lord Jesus, I worry about my family because I love them, but love and worry are not the same thing. I carry adult children, grandchildren, marriages, health, money, faith, and old wounds in my mind as if anxious thought could shepherd them better than You. Have mercy on me. Father, show me what is mine to do and what is mine to release. Holy Spirit, give me watchfulness without meddling, counsel without control, and prayer without despair. If I should speak, make me clear and gentle. If I should be silent, make my silence loving rather than resentful. Teach me to place each name before You as I stand before the icons, trusting the risen Christ with the stories I cannot finish. Keep me from making fear sound like wisdom. Let my next faithful thing be prayer, a kind message, a meal, an apology, or simply rest.
