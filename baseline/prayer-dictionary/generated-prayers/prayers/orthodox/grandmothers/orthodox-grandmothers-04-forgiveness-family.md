# For Old Family Wounds

Lord Jesus Christ, some family wounds have grown old with me. I know the stories so well that I can tell them with all the pauses in the right places. But remembering is not the same as healing, and being right is not the same as being free. Have mercy. I do not ask You to make falsehood true or harm harmless. I ask You to remove revenge, contempt, and the secret pleasure of keeping another person permanently in debt. Father, forgive my sins, including the ones I explain too quickly. Holy Spirit, show me whether repair is possible, whether silence is wise, and whether a word of blessing can be given without pretending. By Your Cross, teach me to release what I cannot collect. By Your Resurrection, make room in me for peace that is stronger than family history. Let my remaining years be marked by mercy, truthful boundaries, and prayer.
