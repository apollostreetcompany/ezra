# For Evening Praise and Rest

Father, evening has come again, and I bring You the day without decorating it. There were mercies I noticed and mercies I missed. There were words I should have spoken, words I should have held back, and small obediences that only You saw. Receive my thanks and my repentance together. Lord Jesus Christ, You are the light no darkness can master. Let praise remain in me even when the house is quiet, the body is tired, or the future feels narrower than it once did. Holy Spirit, teach me to end the day without clinging to it. Bless my family wherever they sleep. Bless the Church keeping prayer across the world. Bless those who are lonely tonight. If I wake in the night, give me the Jesus Prayer instead of fear. If I sleep, let it be in Your mercy. When morning comes, make me ready for another small act of love.
