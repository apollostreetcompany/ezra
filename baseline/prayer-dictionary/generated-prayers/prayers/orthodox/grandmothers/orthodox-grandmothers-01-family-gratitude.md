# For Gratitude Across Generations

Father, thank You for the generations You have allowed me to see. Some days I look at children and grandchildren with wonder; other days I see mistakes, distances, choices I cannot mend, and prayers not yet answered. Teach me gratitude that is truthful. I do not have to pretend every family story is simple in order to bless Your name. Lord Jesus Christ, You gather the scattered and raise the dead; gather what love can still gather in this family. Holy Spirit, make my memory humble. Let me tell the good stories without polishing away the hard ones, and let my words give courage rather than pressure. Thank You for meals shared, birthdays remembered, small phone calls, old photographs, and every sign of grace. Keep me faithful in prayer before the icons and faithful in kindness when people are actually in the room. Let my gratitude become a quiet inheritance of mercy.
