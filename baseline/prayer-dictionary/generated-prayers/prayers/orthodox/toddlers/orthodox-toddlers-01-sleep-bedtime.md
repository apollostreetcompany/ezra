# For a Toddler at Bedtime

Lord Jesus Christ, this little one is worn out, though the body still wiggles and the will still says no. Thank You for the day You gave: play, meals, kisses, and even the hard minutes that taught us patience. In this quiet room, under Your holy Cross, cover all fussing, fear, and anger with mercy. Help this child forgive, be forgiven, and receive sleep as Your gift. Guard this home with Your angels, bless the icons and crib-side prayers, and let Paschal light be stronger than the dark. Wake us to kindness and cheerful obedience. Amen.
