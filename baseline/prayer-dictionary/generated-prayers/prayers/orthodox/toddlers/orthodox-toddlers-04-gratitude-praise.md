# For Toddler Gratitude and Praise

Holy Trinity, thank You for this little child and for today's small mercies: breakfast crumbs, bath water, stories, songs, sturdy shoes, and arms that lift them when they are tired. Teach us to receive these gifts without grabbing, whining, or forgetting You. Let thank you become a simple obedience, not a show. Lord Jesus Christ, bless this home as a little church: icons on the wall, mercy at the table, forgiveness after tears. Holy Spirit, grow wonder, sharing, and glad praise in this child. May the joy of the Resurrection make our ordinary day bright with love. Amen.
