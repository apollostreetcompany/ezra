# For a Sick or Hurting Toddler

Lord Jesus Christ, this dear child is sick or hurting, and we wish we could take every pain away. Please have mercy. Bless the medicine, the doctors, the rest, the water, and every gentle touch. Help this little one feel safe while healing comes. Father, hold their body in Your care. Holy Spirit, give the grownups wisdom and calm. By Your Cross and Resurrection, turn fear toward trust. Let this child be comforted, strengthened, and surrounded by love in the Church and at home.
