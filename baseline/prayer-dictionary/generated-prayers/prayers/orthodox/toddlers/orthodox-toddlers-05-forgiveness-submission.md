# For Toddler Mercy After a Hard Moment

Lord Jesus, we had a hard moment. There may have been hitting, yelling, grabbing, or tears. Help this child learn that sorry is not shame; it is turning back toward love. Help us grownups correct without anger and comfort without giving up. Father, forgive us and teach us. Holy Spirit, grow gentleness and self-control one tiny step at a time. Let this child know mercy in their bones, and let our next hug, cleanup, or kind word become a small beginning again.
