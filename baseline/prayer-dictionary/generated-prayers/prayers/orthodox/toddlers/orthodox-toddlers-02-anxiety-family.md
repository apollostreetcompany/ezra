# For a Toddler Who Feels Afraid

Lord Jesus Christ, this little child is afraid, and fear feels bigger than the room. Thank You that no cry is too small for Your mercy. Help us hold this child without making fear the ruler of the house. Give the grownups calm voices and steady hands. By Your holy Cross, bless this doorway, this bed, this family, and every small brave step: one breath, one hug, one truthful word. Holy Spirit, teach this child to call for help, to receive love, and to obey in trust. Let the joy of Your Resurrection quiet the dark. Amen.
