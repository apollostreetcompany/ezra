# For a Father Offering His Household to God

Father, I begin this day with people depending on me, and I confess that I often confuse responsibility with control. I want to provide, protect, decide, repair, and be respected. Some of that is love; some of it is pride wearing work boots. Lord Jesus Christ, teach me the difference. You are the true Head of this home, not because You dominate, but because You lay down Your life and raise the dead. Holy Spirit, make me watchful over my words, my temper, my appetites, and the hidden bargains I make with comfort. Bless my family through my faithfulness, not through my need to be impressive. Let me pray before I fix, listen before I lecture, and repent before I demand repentance from others. Keep us close to the Church, to the Liturgy, and to mercy. Today, I offer my labor, decisions, interruptions, and limits to You.
