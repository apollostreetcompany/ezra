# For a Father's Gratitude

Holy Trinity, thank You for the life placed around me: children, family, work, meals, noise, questions, repairs, and ordinary days that I often fail to notice until they are gone. I confess that I can treat blessings as interruptions when they arrive wearing muddy shoes or asking for help at the wrong time. Open my eyes. Lord Jesus, You received children and blessed them; teach me to receive my family as gift, not as an obstacle to my plans. Father, thank You for every mercy that keeps us standing: paychecks, forgiveness, laughter, doctors, teachers, the Church, and the quiet help I never counted. Holy Spirit, turn gratitude into attention. Let praise make me less entitled, not merely more emotional. Help me bless my family aloud, thank my wife or co-parent honestly, and use my strength without demanding applause. May our home become a small icon of Your generous love.
