# For a Father Carrying Grief

Lord Jesus Christ, I am grieving, and I do not always know how to do that as a father. I may try to stay useful, stay quiet, stay strong, or stay busy so no one has to see the wound. But hidden grief still teaches the house, and silence can become a wall. Have mercy on me. You wept, and Your tears did not make You less faithful. You entered death and shattered it by Your Resurrection. Father, comfort what is broken in me without making me pretend it did not break. Holy Spirit, give me courage to speak honestly, receive comfort, and comfort others without turning sorrow into control. Heal my body where grief has settled into tiredness, anger, or numbness. Keep me near the prayers of the Church when my own words are thin. Let Paschal hope make me more present today: gentle in speech, faithful in work, and willing to be loved.
