# For a Dad Repenting of Anger

Lord Jesus, I have used anger to make myself feel strong. I may call it discipline, stress, honesty, or being tired, but sometimes it is simply the passion of anger taking command. Show me the truth without letting me collapse into excuses or self-hatred. My family does not need a father who never feels anger; they need one who brings anger under Your mercy. Father, forgive the sharp tone, the slammed door, the cold silence, the look that made someone smaller. Holy Spirit, give me repentance that repairs. Teach me to lower my voice, name my wrong, ask forgiveness, and accept the embarrassment of beginning again. Let the Jesus Prayer interrupt me before my temper becomes a sermon. By Your Cross, crucify the pride that enjoys being feared. By Your Resurrection, make me a man whose strength feels like safety to those entrusted to him.
