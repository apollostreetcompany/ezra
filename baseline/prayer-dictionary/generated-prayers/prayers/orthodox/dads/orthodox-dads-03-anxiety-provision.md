# For Anxiety About Provision

Father, I am anxious about money, work, health, my children's future, and whether I am enough for the people who need me. The fear feels responsible, as if worry itself were a form of provision. But worry cannot become bread, wisdom, or holiness. Lord Jesus Christ, You fed the hungry and also taught us to seek first the Kingdom. Teach me to work diligently without worshiping security. Holy Spirit, show me the next faithful thing: the bill to face, the conversation to have, the habit to change, the prayer to say, the rest to receive. Guard me from escaping into distraction or becoming harsh because I feel powerless. Bless the labor of my hands, and humble me enough to ask for counsel when I need it. I entrust hidden outcomes to Your providence. Keep my family under Your mercy, and make my presence calmer because my trust is in You.
