# For Healing After a Hard Beginning

Lord Jesus, this beginning has not been only sweet. There may be birth pain, fear, surgery, loss, depression, a child in the hospital, milk that did not come, or grief I cannot explain without feeling ungrateful. Help me tell the truth. Gratitude for life does not erase sorrow, and sorrow does not cancel love. You are not embarrassed by my weakness. You entered blood, tears, and death, and You rose with wounds still visible. Father, heal my body, my mind, and my memories at the pace of Your mercy. Holy Spirit, pray in me when I have only sighs. Send wise doctors, faithful friends, and a Church that does not rush me into cheerfulness. Keep me from accusing myself for needing care. By the prayers of the saints who mothered, suffered, and endured, teach me to receive help, hold my child without fear, and hope again without pretending.
