# For Night Feeding and Surrender

Father, night has come, but rest has not come easily. The baby wakes, my body aches, and my thoughts become louder in the dark. I confess that I sometimes want comfort more than love, and control more than trust. Have mercy on me without shaming me for being tired. Lord Jesus, You slept in a boat during a storm and also rose early to pray; teach me the obedience that belongs to this hour, not to an imaginary perfect life. Holy Spirit, make the Jesus Prayer quiet in my heart while I feed, rock, change, and begin again. Help me receive sleep when it is given and not resent the child when it is not. Guard my mind from despair and my tongue from sharpness. Through Your Paschal victory, let these broken nights become hidden offerings, and when I finally lie down, let me rest in Your mercy.
