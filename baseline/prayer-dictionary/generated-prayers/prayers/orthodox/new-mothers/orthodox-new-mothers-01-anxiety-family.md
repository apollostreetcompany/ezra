# For a New Mother's Worry

Lord Jesus Christ, I am a new mother, and love has made me more tender than I knew I could be. It has also made me afraid. I watch breathing, feeding, sleep, development, my own body, and everyone else's advice. Some attention is care; some is the fantasy that I can keep this child alive by never resting. Teach me the difference. Father, this child is entrusted to me, but not owned by me. Holy Spirit, give me watchfulness without panic, and humility to ask for help before I am empty. Bless the family around us; heal what is strained, and make our home gentle. Let icons, prayers, lullabies, diapers, and dishes all become places where grace can be received. By the mercy of the risen Christ, keep me from measuring motherhood by control. Give me enough light for the next feeding, the next apology, the next nap, the next small act of love.
