# For Forgiveness in a Changed Household

Lord Jesus Christ, a new baby has changed the whole household, and not all of us have changed gracefully. I am quicker to notice who did not help, who gave advice badly, who slept, who criticized, who disappeared, or who needed more from me than I could give. Some wounds need honest speech; some complaints are only exhaustion looking for a target. Give me discernment. You forgive without pretending evil is good, and You tell the truth without cruelty. Father, forgive my sharp words, my silent punishments, and my pride in being the one who suffers most. Holy Spirit, heal the passions that grow in tired families: resentment, scorekeeping, self-pity, and contempt. Help me ask plainly for what is needed, receive help without suspicion, and offer mercy without erasing boundaries. Let our home become a small school of repentance. By Your Resurrection, make forgiveness possible before the feeling is easy.
