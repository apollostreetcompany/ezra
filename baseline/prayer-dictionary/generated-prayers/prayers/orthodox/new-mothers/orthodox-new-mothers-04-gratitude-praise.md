# For Wonder Over a New Child

Holy Trinity, I am holding a life I did not create from nothing and cannot fully understand. This child is ordinary in the way all babies are ordinary: hungry, sleepy, loud, soft, and needy. This child is also a mystery received from You. Keep my gratitude sane and embodied. Let me praise You while folding laundry, washing bottles, taking medicine, asking questions, and smiling at tiny expressions no one else may notice. Lord Jesus, bless this child as Your beloved creature, called not merely to be successful, but to become alive in You. Father, thank You for the people who help, for food, shelter, skillful care, and for the Church that receives both mother and child. Holy Spirit, turn wonder into patience and praise into service. Do not let me grasp this gift as if fear could protect it. Teach me to offer this child back to You with open hands and a steady heart.
