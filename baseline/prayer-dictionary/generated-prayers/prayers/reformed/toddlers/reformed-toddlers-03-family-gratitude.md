# A Family Gratitude Prayer With a Toddler

Father, thank You for this child and for the family You have placed around this little life. Every good meal, clean shirt, silly laugh, shared toy, and helping hand is from You. Teach us not to act as if these gifts belong to us by right. We receive them by grace. Help this child learn love through our words, our patience, and our repentance. Keep our home under Christ's kindness. Make today a day for simple obedience: sharing, listening, forgiving, and giving thanks. Amen.
