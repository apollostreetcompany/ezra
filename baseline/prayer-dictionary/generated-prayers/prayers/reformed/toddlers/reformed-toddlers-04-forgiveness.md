# A Simple Prayer for Saying Sorry

Father, we need Your mercy when we grab, shout, hit, hide, or refuse to listen. Help this little child learn that saying sorry is not only escaping trouble; it is turning back toward what is good. Thank You that Jesus is a strong Savior for sinners, even very small ones. Give a soft heart, honest words, and willing hands to make things right. Help us adults show forgiveness clearly and patiently. Lead this child in grace, one small step at a time. Amen.
