# A Prayer When a Little One Feels Afraid

Father, this little one feels afraid, and fear can feel very big in a small body. Help us not to scold fear as if it were nothing, and not to obey it as if it were king. You are Lord over the dark, the noise, the new place, and the hard moment. Thank You that Jesus receives weak and needy people. Give this child calm breath, a quiet heart, and courage for one small step. Help us speak gently and trust Your providence together. Amen.
