# A Little Prayer of Praise and Trust

Father, this little child is small, and You are very great. You made the morning, the stars, the hands that help us, and the voices that sing. Thank You that we do not need big words to come to You through Jesus. Help this child learn to praise You with a glad heart and to obey with a willing heart. When the answer is no, when waiting is hard, and when help is needed, teach trust. Rule this little life with Your wise and loving care. Amen.
