# A Bedtime Prayer Over a Little Child

Father, this little child is sleepy, and the day has been full. Thank You for small mercies: food, play, hugs, help, and safe moments we did not even notice. Forgive our impatience and all the little ways our hearts turned from You. Because of Jesus, we come by grace, not by being perfect. Please guard this child through the night. Quiet fears, rest this body, and make our home gentle. Teach this little one, day by day, to trust Your good care. Amen.
