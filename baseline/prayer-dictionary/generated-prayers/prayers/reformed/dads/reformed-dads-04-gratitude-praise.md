# A Prayer Against Proud Self-Reliance

Father, when work goes well, I am tempted to call it mine. I can look at income, skill, endurance, and problem-solving as if I produced myself. Teach me the difference between honest effort and proud self-reliance. Every ability I use, every opportunity I receive, every meal on our table, and every breath in my lungs comes from You. Forgive me for praising my discipline more readily than Your mercy. In Christ, I have nothing to boast in except grace. Make gratitude more than a polite thought before dinner. Let it become generosity, patient speech, open hands, and worship when no one is impressed. Holy Spirit, free me from the need to be seen as the source. Let my children and neighbors find me thankful, not entitled. Teach me to receive limits without resentment and success without arrogance. I praise You as Creator, Redeemer, and Provider, and I offer today's work back to You. Amen.
