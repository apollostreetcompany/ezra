# A Prayer for Repenting After Harsh Words

Lord Jesus, I have spoken as if volume could create righteousness. I have used irritation, sarcasm, silence, or authority to avoid the harder work of love. Teach me the difference between being firm and being harsh, between correcting sin and feeding my own anger. I confess that I often want obedience from others more than holiness in myself. Forgive me by grace alone. Do not let me hide behind stress, fatigue, or the claim that I meant well. True repentance turns and walks another way. Unite me to You, the meek and faithful Savior, so that my strength becomes governed by mercy. Help me ask forgiveness plainly from the people I have hurt, especially when they are younger or weaker than I am. Give me patience to rebuild trust by repeated faithfulness. Holy Spirit, sanctify my mouth, my face, and my tone. Make my home safer because I am under Your discipline. Amen.
