# A Prayer for a Father in Grief

Father, I do not always know how to grieve without shutting down. I may try to fix what cannot be fixed, stay busy so I do not feel, or act strong because others need me. Teach me the difference between courage and numbness. You are sovereign over this sorrow, yet You are not distant from it. Christ has borne grief without sin, and those united to Him are not abandoned in the valley. Forgive me for the pride that refuses comfort and the fear that refuses tears. Heal what is wounded in me and in those I love. Where healing does not come quickly, give endurance shaped by faith. Help me receive comfort from Your Word, Your Spirit, and Your people, then give comfort without pretending I have mastered pain. Lead me in the next faithful thing: presence, prayer, honest speech, or quiet service. Hold our family in Your providence until hope becomes visible again. Amen.
