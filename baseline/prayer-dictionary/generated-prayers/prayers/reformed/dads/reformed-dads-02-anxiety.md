# A Prayer for a Father Carrying Worry

Father, I carry worries I do not always name. Bills, work, my children's future, my marriage, aging parents, and my own failures can sit on my chest like they are mine to govern. Teach me the difference between responsibility and unbelieving control. You have called me to provide, protect, repent, and serve; You have not called me to be sovereign. Forgive me when fear makes me irritable, secretive, or proud. Through Christ alone, remind me that my worth is not measured by income, competence, or the appearance of having everything handled. Give me faith for the next faithful thing: a budget, a phone call, a prayer with my wife, a hard truth, a doctor's appointment, or a quiet hour with my child. Holy Spirit, make my courage obedient rather than loud. I cast the outcomes I cannot govern onto Your providence and receive today as a trust from You. Amen.
