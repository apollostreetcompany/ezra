# A Prayer for Leading the Family Humbly

Father, I can confuse leading my family with getting my own way. I can also hide from responsibility and call it peacekeeping. Teach me the difference between humble headship, faithful service, and selfish control. This home belongs to You before it belongs to me. Christ is the true Shepherd, and I am answerable for the way I speak, decide, apologize, provide, discipline, and pray. Forgive me for harshness, passivity, image-making, and the pride that resists correction. Make me a man who receives grace before trying to give wisdom. Help me lead by repentance as well as instruction. Give me patience for small interruptions, courage for hard conversations, and tenderness that is not weakness. Holy Spirit, sanctify my work and my presence at home. Let my children see a father under authority, not a father pretending to be the authority. I submit this household to Your covenant faithfulness and ask for today's nearest obedience. Amen.
