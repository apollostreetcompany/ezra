# A Prayer for Forgiveness in a Complicated Family

Father, family history can become a room I keep walking back into. Old words, unfair choices, disappointments, and griefs may still speak loudly in me. Teach me the difference between remembering truthfully and rehearsing resentment. You do not ask me to call evil good, and You do not permit me to nourish bitterness as if it were justice. I confess the ways I have kept score, sharpened stories, or used silence to punish. Forgive me through Christ, who bore my sins without becoming like them. Give me grace to will the good of those I cannot easily trust. Show me what repair is mine to pursue and what outcome belongs to You. Holy Spirit, guard my speech when family gathers. Make me quick to bless, slow to accuse, and wise about boundaries. Let forgiveness in me be neither denial nor revenge, but obedience shaped by the mercy I have received. Amen.
