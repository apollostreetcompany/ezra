# An Evening Prayer for Grief and Gratitude

Father, evening brings memories with it. Some are sweet, some ache, and some arrive without asking permission. Teach me the difference between gratitude that remembers Your gifts and grief that tries to make the past my home. Thank You for faces, voices, places, meals, births, prayers, and ordinary days I did not know I would miss. Thank You also for mercy that carried me through sins, losses, and changes I could not carry myself. Forgive me where nostalgia becomes unbelief or where sorrow makes me forget the resurrection promised in Christ. I entrust the dead, the living, and the unfinished stories to Your righteous and tender hands. Holy Spirit, comfort me without making me careless, and keep me thankful without making me shallow. As I sleep, guard my mind from regret and my heart from fear. Let tonight's rest be a small confession that You remain God, and tomorrow, if You give it, belongs to You. Amen.
