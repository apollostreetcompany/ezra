# A Prayer for Praising God Across Generations

Father, I have seen enough years to know that families are not held together by my worry or wisdom. Children grow, grandchildren change, decisions are made beyond my reach, and my advice is not always wanted. Teach me the difference between faithful influence and anxious control. You have been covenantally kind through more seasons than I can count. Thank You for mercies remembered and mercies I missed at the time. Forgive me for praising You with my mouth while trying to manage everyone with my hands. Let my life commend Christ without pressure or pretense. Give me words that are timely, prayers that are persistent, and silence that is loving when silence is best. Holy Spirit, make my home, calls, notes, meals, and stories instruments of grace. Let the younger ones see not a perfect woman, but a forgiven one. I praise You for Your faithfulness from generation to generation and submit my family to Your care. Amen.
