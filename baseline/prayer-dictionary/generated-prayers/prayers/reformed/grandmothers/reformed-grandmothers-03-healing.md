# A Prayer for Healing and Perseverance

Lord Jesus, my body reminds me that I am a creature. Pain, weakness, appointments, medicines, and limitations can make the day feel smaller than it once did. Teach me the difference between asking for healing and demanding that life return to my preferred form. You are able to heal, and Your providence is wise when healing is slow. Forgive me for resentment when I cannot do what I used to do, and for pride when I refuse needed help. In union with You, my outward weakness is not the measure of my usefulness. Give doctors skill, treatments mercy, and my body strength according to Your will. Holy Spirit, sanctify my limitations so they produce patience, prayer, and tenderness rather than complaint. Let me receive care without shame and offer care without keeping score. Whether You restore much or little, keep my faith resting on Christ alone and my hope fixed beyond this present affliction. Amen.
