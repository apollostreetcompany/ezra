# A Prayer for Releasing What I Cannot Control

Father, I can see troubles coming and imagine more besides. Age has taught me that life can change quickly, but fear uses that lesson badly. Teach me the difference between wisdom gained through years and anxiety dressed up as wisdom. You are sovereign over my children, grandchildren, health, finances, church, and unfinished concerns. I am not. Forgive me for acting as if repeated worry were a form of love. Because I am in Christ, I may pray boldly and release outcomes humbly. Give me the next faithful thing: a phone call, a written encouragement, a practical errand, a doctor's visit, a quiet prayer, or the choice not to interfere. Holy Spirit, steady my mind when old fears return with new names. Make submission to Your providence peaceful, not passive. Let my trust become a gift to those around me, so that I do not hand them my fear when I mean to give them love. Amen.
