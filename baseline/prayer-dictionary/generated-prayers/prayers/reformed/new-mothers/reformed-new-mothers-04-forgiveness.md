# A Prayer for Forgiveness When I Am Frayed

Lord Jesus, tiredness has shown me parts of my heart I would rather not see. I can excuse sharp words because I am exhausted, envy others because they seem rested, and resent the very people I love. Teach me the difference between honest limits and sin I have renamed as necessity. You know my frame, but You do not flatter my pride. Search me with mercy. Forgive me, not because I have explained myself well, but because Your blood is enough for sinners. Unite me to You so repentance becomes possible and not merely another burden. Help me confess specifically where I have wounded someone. Give me courage to ask forgiveness without performing misery, and humility to receive it without defending myself. Holy Spirit, sanctify this hidden season. Make me more gentle, truthful, and dependent. Let grace alone be my hope, and let that grace become visible in my next word. Amen.
