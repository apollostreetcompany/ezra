# A Prayer for an Anxious New Mother

Father, I am tired in places I did not know could be tired. My body is healing, my mind is crowded, and a small cry can make my heart race. Teach me the difference between faithful care and the belief that I must control every breath, feeding, nap, symptom, and future outcome. This child is not finally held together by my alertness, but by Your providence. I confess my fear, and also my pride in thinking love means never needing help. Thank You that Christ is sufficient for mothers who are weak, not only for mothers who seem composed. Heal my body in Your mercy. Guard my mind from despair and false guilt. Give me grace for the next faithful thing: to feed, rest, call the doctor, ask for help, pray, or let someone serve us. Holy Spirit, make my weakness a place where faith becomes more honest. Keep this child and me under Your covenant kindness. Amen.
