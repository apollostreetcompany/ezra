# A Prayer for Submitting Family Life to God

Father, this family is not my little kingdom. I forget that quickly. I can turn schedules, feeding, sleep, visitors, advice, and household rhythms into proof that I am either succeeding or failing. Teach me the difference between wise stewardship and anxious ownership. Unless You build the house, my effort cannot make it whole. Forgive me for using control to cover fear. Give me grace to receive this child, this home, and this season as gifts entrusted by You. Through Christ, make my ordinary work holy: washing, feeding, answering, deciding, waiting, and welcoming help. Help me honor my husband, family, church, and neighbors without surrendering to every opinion. Holy Spirit, form in me patience, clarity, and quiet courage. Let our home be shaped by Word, prayer, repentance, and joy, not by image management. I submit today's needs to Your providence and ask for faithfulness in the nearest duty. Amen.
