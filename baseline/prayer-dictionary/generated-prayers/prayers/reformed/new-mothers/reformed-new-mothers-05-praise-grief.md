# A Prayer for Praise When Motherhood Includes Grief

Father, motherhood has brought joy, but it has also uncovered grief. Some losses are obvious, and some are harder to name: freedom, former strength, a hoped-for birth, an easy bond, a child not here, or a self I thought I understood. Teach me the difference between lament and unbelief. You do not require me to praise You by pretending sorrow is small. You are sovereign, and You are near to the brokenhearted. Thank You that Christ entered suffering and now intercedes for those joined to Him. Receive my weak praise as faith, not as a performance. Where grief has made me numb, keep me close to Your Word. Where joy comes, let me receive it without guilt. Where healing is slow, give endurance. Holy Spirit, pray in my weakness and turn my heart toward the Father. Let my praise be honest, reverent, and rooted in grace that cannot be taken away. Amen.
