# A Night Prayer After a Long Day With the Baby

Father, this day feels scattered in my hands. I remember unfinished chores, a short temper, small comforts, and minutes of wonder I almost missed. Teach me the difference between reviewing the day truthfully and measuring my worth by how smoothly it went. Where I sinned in impatience or resentment, forgive me through Christ alone and lead me to repair. Where I served in hidden ways, keep me from needing applause. Thank You for milk, diapers, sleep when it came, hands that helped, and the strange mercy of being needed. Your faithfulness did not depend on my calmness. Tonight I entrust this child, this body, this home, and the next waking to You. If sleep is short, give grace. If sleep is long, give gratitude. Holy Spirit, quiet my thoughts and sanctify even the interruptions. Let me rest as a daughter before I rise again as a mother. Amen.
