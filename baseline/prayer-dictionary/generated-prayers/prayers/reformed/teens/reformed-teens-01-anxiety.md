# A Prayer When Worry Feels Bigger Than School

Father, my thoughts can run ahead of me before the day has even started. School, friendships, my body, my future, my failures, and what people think of me all try to sound like final authorities. Teach me the difference between concern that should make me faithful and fear that tries to make me its servant. You are sovereign over what I can see and what I cannot see. Because I belong to Christ, my life is not held together by my performance or popularity, but by Your grace. Forgive me for treating anxiety as if it were wiser than Your Word. Give me enough trust for the next faithful thing: to study, apologize, ask for help, tell the truth, put down my phone, or rest. Holy Spirit, steady my mind and make my faith active, not dramatic. I entrust the unknown to Your providence and receive this hour from Your hand. Amen.
