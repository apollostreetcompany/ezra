# A Bedtime Prayer for Surrender

Father, the day is over, but my mind still wants to manage it. I replay conversations, imagine better answers, defend myself, and worry about tomorrow as if I could repair the world by staying awake. Teach me the difference between honest reflection and restless control. Search me without crushing me. Where I sinned, lead me to repentance and to the repair that belongs to tomorrow. Where I was helped, make me thankful. Where I was misunderstood, keep me from building a private courtroom in my head. I rest tonight because Christ intercedes for me, not because I have finished everything well. Your covenant faithfulness does not pause while I sleep. Holy Spirit, quiet my body and my thoughts under the Father’s care. I submit unfinished work, unanswered texts, hidden fears, and tomorrow’s duties to Your providence. Let sleep be an act of faith, and wake me ready for simple obedience. Amen.
