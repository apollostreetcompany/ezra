# A Prayer for Gratitude Without Pretending

Father, I do not always feel grateful. Some days I notice what I lack faster than what You have given. I compare my face, my family, my talents, my clothes, my grades, and my plans, and then I call the result honesty. Teach me the difference between wanting good gifts and demanding a life arranged around me. Every breath, friendship, meal, ability, correction, and ordinary mercy comes from Your hand. More than all these, You have given Christ, and in Him You have given grace I could never earn. Forgive me for praising You only when my life feels easy. Let gratitude become obedience: careful words, diligent work, clean thoughts, and kindness where I would rather complain. Holy Spirit, train my attention to see providence in common things without pretending pain is not real. Receive this day from me as worship through Christ alone. Amen.
