# A Prayer for Forgiving at Home

Lord Jesus, home can be the place where my worst words come out first. I can be quick to remember what was unfair and slow to notice how I have wounded others. Teach me the difference between pretending everything is fine and truly seeking peace. You do not ask me to invent warm feelings on command, but You do command me to put away bitterness, pride, and revenge. I confess that I often want to win the room more than I want to love my family. Forgive me by Your finished work, not because my apology is impressive. Unite me more deeply to You, so that Your mercy becomes the pattern of my speech. Help me take one honest step today: a quiet answer, a real apology, a patient silence, or a willingness to listen. Keep me from using forgiveness to excuse sin, and keep me from using justice to hide malice. Make our home a place where grace is practiced. Amen.
