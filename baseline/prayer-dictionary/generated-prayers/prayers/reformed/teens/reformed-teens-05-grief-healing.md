# A Prayer When Hurt Does Not Make Sense

Lord Jesus, I do not know what to do with this sadness. Part of me wants to be numb, and another part wants an answer that would make the pain behave. Teach me the difference between trusting You and pretending that grief is small. You were not surprised by this loss, this sickness, or this wound, yet Your sovereignty is not cold. You have joined Your people to Christ, and He carries us through suffering toward resurrection. Forgive me when I treat my present feeling as the whole truth about You. Give me grace to lament without accusing You of evil, and to hope without rushing myself or others. Heal what You will in the way You will. Strengthen faith where healing is slow. Help me do the next faithful thing: eat, sleep, ask for prayer, tell the truth, or sit quietly before You. Keep me near Your Word and Your people until joy is renewed. Amen.
